### 3.5 Certification: recovery scenario + recheck, planned at its real size (blockers 2 & 3)

**Shape.** Turn vocabulary becomes `'discover' | 'approve' | 'record-only' | 'recover'`. Two scenarios as harness constants — `SCENARIO_TURNS = { standard: ['discover','approve'], recovery: ['record-only','recover','approve'] }`, `PASS_SCENARIOS = ['standard','standard','recovery']` — so passes 1-2 stay the standard proof and pass 3 becomes the recovery variant. This is the contract-variant design: turn *semantics* are keyed by turn **name** everywhere (expectations, schemas, prompts, terminal phases, normalization), and the only ordinal-keyed thing left is which scenario a pass runs.
